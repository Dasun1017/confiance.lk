Important Operational Notice: Training vs. Employment Decisions

Admission to a course does not itself ensure overseas employment, a work permit, or a visa. CONFIANCE operates strictly as an educational and technical preparation institution. Overseas career opportunities are eligibility- and approval-dependent: final employer selection, work permits, and visa grants rest exclusively with authorized foreign employers and sovereign immigration authorities. Where formal recruitment placement is required, it is managed through licensed recruitment entities (such as the Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Agency · SLFEA) in full accordance with SLBFE regulations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Facts About Career Pathways

Straightforward answers regarding our training role, licensed placement procedures, and realistic expectations.

Does enrolling in a CONFIANCE course guarantee an overseas job or visa?

No. We are completely transparent with our students: admission to a language or vocational course builds essential eligibility and candidate readiness, but it does NOT guarantee an overseas job, work permit, or visa. Job offers are decided exclusively by prospective overseas employers, and visas are granted solely by sovereign foreign immigration authorities.

What is the difference between CONFIANCE and a foreign employment agency?

CONFIANCE is an educational and technical preparation academy. We deliver language instruction, practical workshop trade orientation, CV formatting, and interview preparation. Regulated overseas recruitment—such as processing official job orders, contract signing, and visa filing—is performed exclusively by authorized, licensed foreign employment agencies (such as the state-owned Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Agency · SLFEA) under the supervision of the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE).

Why is Japan featured as the primary focus pathway?

Japan represents one of the most structured, high-demand, and transparent government-regulated technical tracks through the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) and Technical Intern Training Program (TITP). Sectors such as Caregiver (Kaigo), Airport Ground Handling, Food & Beverage Manufacturing, and Agriculture have well-defined language benchmarks (JLPT/JFT) and recognized skill test exams, allowing candidates to prepare systematically.

How does the France butcher and technical recruitment pathway operate?

Under the coordinated France recruitment package, CONFIANCE conducts the intensive candidate preparation: French language communication (DELF A1/A2), knife mastery, food processing ergonomics, and EU hygiene/HACCP standards. SLFEA acts as the statutory licensed recruitment agency, handling verified employer job orders, candidate contracts, and French consular visa lodgement.

Can I switch between country pathways if my plans change?

Yes. During your initial Discover and Assess stages, our career counselors will review your personal profile. If you decide that another destination (e.g. pivoting from Japan to Korea or France) better aligns with your skills or intake timelines, our team will assist you in transitioning to the appropriate language and training cohort.
