Transparent Operational Distinction: Training vs. Licensed Recruitment

CONFIANCE Language & Vocational Training School is a dedicated educational and vocational training institution. We do not operate as an employment agency or issue overseas work visas. For international employment packages (including our France recruitment packages, Japan SSW, and Korea EPS pathways), Confiance serves exclusively as the technical, linguistic, and professional preparation partner, while the Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Agency (SLFEA) and authorized licensed agencies perform the statutory recruitment agent role, contract validation, and official government processing under SLBFE regulations.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Partnership Governance & Operational Standards

Clear answers regarding operational scope, legal distinctions, and institutional collaboration standards at Confiance.

Does CONFIANCE directly recruit or issue foreign employment contracts?

No. Confiance Language & Vocational Training School is an educational training school providing intensive foreign language classes, vocational trade orientation, and candidate interview readiness. All official overseas employment orders, candidate submissions, employment contracts, and migration visa processing are performed exclusively by authorized licensed foreign employment agencies—such as our state-owned partner the Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Agency (SLFEA)—under full registration with the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE).

How does the France recruitment package work between Confiance and SLFEA?

For the France technical recruitment packages (such as Butcher and Hospitality tracks), Confiance provides the rigorous preparatory training: French DELF A1/A2 language training, knife-handling technique, food hygiene (HACCP) principles, and cultural orientation. Once candidates achieve required technical readiness, SLFEA—as the statutory licensed recruitment agency—manages the official employer interviews, contract verification, work authorization filing, and visa lodgement with French authorities.

Are all institutional partnerships formalized through written MoUs or agreements?

Yes. Confiance operates under strict compliance guidelines. We name counterparties, public authorities, and institutional collaborators only where active agreements, documented scopes of work, and approved permissions exist. Private contractual terms and sensitive candidate data are never published online.

Can overseas employers directly sponsor training batches at Confiance?

Yes. Foreign employers and supervising organizations (e.g. Japanese AO/SO organizations, European hospitality chains) can sponsor customized training cohorts. We customize the vocabulary syllabus, practical work simulations, and testing benchmarks to match your exact standard operating procedures (SOPs).

Does Confiance work with sub-agents or informal brokers?

Strictly no. Confiance enforces a zero-tolerance policy against informal brokers and unregistered intermediaries. Candidates enroll directly with Confiance, pay published educational fees, and interact directly with accredited licensed recruitment partners (SLFEA). This ensures full financial protection, legal integrity, and transparency for every student.
