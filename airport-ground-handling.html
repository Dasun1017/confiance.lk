Airport Ground Handling Training

Prepare for international aviation ground services: passenger check-in terminal procedures, boarding coordination, baggage sorting protocols, ramp safety standards, and customer service communication in Japanese and English.

Apply for Airport Training View Japan Pathway
Course Snapshot
  • Course Duration2.5 Months (120 Hours)
  • Training LocationNegombo Aviation Suite
  • Target FrameworkJapan SSW Airport Services
  • Language RequisiteJLPT N4 or JFT-Basic A2
  • Safety ProtocolAirside Ramp Safety Drill
  • CertificateConfiance Aviation Readiness
WhatsApp Course Advisor
Notice: Confiance conducts training and readiness preparation. Placement with airport ground operators is managed strictly through licensed statutory recruitment partners (such as SLFEA). Admission to this course does not ensure an overseas work permit or visa.

Curriculum & Practical Modules

  • Module 01: Terminal Passenger Services & Check-In

    Check-in desk simulations, travel document verification, boarding pass issuance, baggage allowance checks, and special assistance handling.

  • Module 02: Gate Operations & Boarding Procedures

    Gate announcement delivery, electronic boarding gate scanning, boarding priority management, and managing passenger flight delays.

  • Module 03: Baggage Sorting & Handling Systems

    Baggage tag decoding, conveyor belt loading safety, lost & found property documentation, and baggage handling systems.

  • Module 04: Ramp Area & Airside Safety Standards

    Airside PPE usage (high-visibility vests, hearing protection), FOD (Foreign Object Debris) prevention, marshalling signals, and aircraft perimeter safety.

  • Module 05: Aviation Japanese Vocabulary & Etiquette

    Airport customer service Japanese (Keigo), respectful passenger greetings, explaining baggage rules, and formal radio communication codes.

Practical Lab Equipment

  • Simulated airport check-in counter desk
  • Barcode scanner & boarding pass verification system
  • Aviation ramp high-visibility protective gear
  • Aviation sound simulation & headset equipment
  • Baggage tag printer & weight measurement station

Learner Eligibility & Prerequisites

  • Minimum Age: 18+ years
  • Education: G.C.E. A/L or equivalent
  • Good interpersonal communication & customer care orientation
  • Concurrent enrollment in Japanese Language N4/N3 recommended
