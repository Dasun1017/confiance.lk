Agriculture & Greenhouse Farming Training

Gain practical, job-ready skills in climate-controlled greenhouse agriculture, hydroponic crop management, irrigation automation, harvest sorting, and Japanese & Korean farming workplace terminology.

Curriculum & Practical Modules

  • Module 01: Controlled Environment Agriculture & Greenhouse Systems

    Structure of modern poly-tunnels and greenhouses, climate automation, automated ventilation vents, shade netting, and temperature/humidity sensor maintenance.

  • Module 02: Soil Preparation, Hydroponics & Drip Irrigation

    Substrate mixing, soil bed sterilization, hydroponic nutrient film technique (NFT), electrical conductivity (EC) measuring, and drip irrigation scheduling.

  • Module 03: Nursery Management, Pruning & Crop Care

    Seed germination, seedling tray transplantation, plant training, tomato/cucumber trellising, lateral sucker pruning, and integrated pest monitoring (IPM).

  • Module 04: Harvest Grading, Packaging & Cold Storage

    Maturity indices, damage-free picking techniques, size and color grading standards, field packing into export crates, and cold chain transfer routines.

  • Module 05: Farm Machine Safety, PPE & Japanese/Korean Terms

    Safe operation of walking rotary tillers, motorized sprayers, agricultural PPE usage, chemical storage safety, and essential Japanese (Nougyou) & Korean farming phrases.

Practical Farm Equipment

  • Covered demonstration greenhouse with shade netting
  • Hydroponic trough systems & automated drip emitters
  • Digital soil pH, EC and moisture measurement meters
  • Backpack pressure sprayers & certified agricultural PPE suits
  • Ergonomic harvesting shears, crates & precision scales

Learner Eligibility & Prerequisites

  • Minimum Age: 18+ years
  • Education: G.C.E. O/L or equivalent
  • Physical fitness and readiness for farm workshop routines
  • Japanese N5/N4 or Korean EPS-TOPIK foundation recommended
