PURPOSE & SCOPE

Build Authentic French Fluency for Study, Daily Life & Employment

French is an official language across 29 countries and international diplomatic institutions. Our program is tailored to give you genuine oral spontaneity, accurate pronunciation, and the structural grounding needed to thrive in Francophone destinations.

Target Audience

  • Students preparing for undergraduate or postgraduate studies in France, Belgium, or Switzerland.
  • Skilled professionals targeting Canadian permanent residency (Quebec / Express Entry Francophone bonus points).
  • Candidates seeking employment in France, international hospitality, or global aviation sectors.
  • Beginners eager to speak one of the world's most influential cultural languages.

Practical Learning Outcomes

  • Solid foundation in French phonetics, silent letters, and liaisons for clear articulation.
  • Everyday conversation: introductions, polite requests, dining out, directions, and shopping.
  • Essential grammar: present tense regular and irregular verbs, passé composé, and near future (futur proche).
  • Preparation for DELF exam components: listening comprehension, reading, written expression, and oral interview.
DELF CURRICULUM

Course Scope, Modules & Learning Track

Structured around official Centre international d'études pédagogiques (CIEP) DELF guidelines.

DELF A1

French DELF A1 (Discovery)

Complete beginner track. Learn French pronunciation, basic vocabulary, and survive routine everyday situations.

  • French sounds, vowels (nasal sounds), and liaison rules
  • Noun genders (masculin/féminin), definite and indefinite articles
  • Key verbs: être, avoir, aller, faire, and regular -er verbs
  • Introducing oneself, family, hobbies, and simple daily routine
  • DELF A1 model listening and speaking mock interviews
Duration: 12 Weeks Hours: 120 Hours
DELF A2

French DELF A2 (Survival)

Pre-intermediate level. Build independence in conversational exchanges, narrative writing, and practical tasks.

  • Past tense (Passé Composé with avoir & être agreements)
  • Introduction to Imparfait and future tenses
  • Expressing agreement, disagreement, obligations, and polite advice
  • Writing informal emails, letters of invitation, and booking inquiries
  • Intensive past-paper simulations for DELF A2 examination
Duration: 14 Weeks Hours: 140 Hours
CAREER TRACK

Workplace & Canada Migration Track

Targeted for candidates aiming for French work settings or TEF / TCF Canada preparation benchmarks.

  • Professional workplace greetings, phone etiquette, and email correspondence
  • Industry vocabulary: culinary, hotel operations, and retail customer service
  • TEF / TCF examination format breakdown and scoring techniques
  • Formal French job interview role-playing and CV drafting
  • Cultural orientation for working in France and Quebec
Duration: 10 Weeks Hours: 100 Hours

FRENCH FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions: French Program

Useful insights about studying French at Confiance.

Is French pronunciation difficult to master?
French pronunciation has specific silent letters and nasal vowels that can feel unfamiliar initially. However, French spelling rules are very regular. Our specialized phonetic modules break down the mouth positions and liaison patterns step by step, allowing students to speak with confidence.
Does this program help with Canadian immigration points?
Yes! Demonstrating French language proficiency through exams like TEF Canada or TCF Canada can grant significant additional CRS points in Canada's Express Entry and is a primary requirement for Quebec immigration tracks.
Are mock DELF examinations included?
Yes. Each level includes at least two full mock tests replicating the exact time limits, listening tracks, and scoring criteria used in official DELF exams.
