Finder
------
Countries: Japan, Italy, Romania, Canada, France.
Age supports typing and plus/minus controls. The 1-100 input range is form
validation only and does not determine employment or visa eligibility.
Grade currently means highest educational qualification (O/L, A/L, Diploma,
Degree and school grades). Gender is optional and is not used to exclude or
rank people. There are no invented job listings or matching/eligibility rules.

Until confirmed vacancy data is supplied, Find Matching Jobs creates a
transparent pathway enquiry with a notice that no matches are published here.
The profile can be shared by the visitor through a pre-filled WhatsApp message
or their own email client. The website does not automatically send or store
these finder values, and no new application-email backend is used.
