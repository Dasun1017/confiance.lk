Butchery & Meat Processing Training

A specialized, practical vocational program preparing candidates for the European meat processing sector—with a particular focus on France vocational pathways. Learn professional carcass breakdown, precision deboning, cold chain protocols, food safety (HACCP), and workplace French communication.

Curriculum & Practical Modules

  • Module 01: Professional Knife Skills & PPE Safety

    Proper grip techniques, honing and sharpening knives, compulsory usage of stainless steel chainmail gloves, cut-resistant aprons, and non-slip safety boots.

  • Module 02: Carcass Breakdown, Deboning & Trimming

    Systematic deboning of primal and sub-primal cuts (beef, pork, poultry), fat trimming, silver-skin removal, and portioning techniques according to European commercial standards.

  • Module 03: HACCP Food Hygiene & Sanitization

    Cross-contamination prevention, sanitizing cutting blocks and equipment, personal hygiene standards, chemical safety, and international food handling standards.

  • Module 04: Cold Chain Management & Storage

    Temperature maintenance in chillers and freezers, vacuum packing techniques, labeling compliance, shelf-life management, and defrosting protocols.

  • Module 05: French Meat Processing Terminology

    Essential French culinary vocabulary for cuts (entrecôte, filet, bavette), workshop tools, measurements, safety instructions, and reporting to floor supervisors.

  • Module 06: Speed, Dexterity & Ergonomics

    Repetitive motion endurance, correct standing posture to reduce fatigue, conveyor-line pace adaptation, and quality inspection checks.

Practical Lab Equipment

  • Food-grade stainless steel butchery tables
  • Professional boning, skinning & carving knives
  • Diamond sharpening steels & whetstones
  • Stainless steel chainmail safety gloves
  • Heavy-duty food-grade aprons & arm guards
  • Vacuum packaging & portion-weighing stations

Learner Eligibility & Prerequisites

  • Minimum Age: 18+ years (Male candidates preferred for standard European butchery quotas)
  • Education: G.C.E. O/L or equivalent
  • Manual dexterity, stamina & good physical health
  • Concurrent enrollment in French Language A1/A2 required
