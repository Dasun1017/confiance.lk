COURSE PURPOSE & OUTCOMES

Designed for Candidates Pursuing Higher Studies & Japan Work Visas

Whether your goal is securing a student visa for Japanese language institutes, qualifying for Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) categories (Caregiver, Airport Ground Handling, Agriculture, Food Service), or Technical Intern Training (TITP), our program prepares you with practical fluency and exam competence.

Who This Course is For

  • Candidates preparing for Japan student visa applications requiring 150+ verified study hours.
  • Job seekers aiming for SSW Specified Skilled Worker visas (Caregiver, Airport, Food Processing).
  • TITP technical intern candidates needing workplace safety and conversational Japanese.
  • Professionals and language enthusiasts looking to build authentic Japanese speaking fluency.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Complete mastery of Hiragana, Katakana, and required Kanji for each target level.
  • Confident everyday conversation: self-introductions, shopping, healthcare, and directions.
  • Japanese workplace etiquette (Keigo polite forms, bowing, business greetings, punctuality).
  • Comprehensive exam paper technique for JLPT listening, grammar, and reading sections.
LEVEL PATHWAYS

Course Levels, Syllabus & Modules

Follow a progressive curriculum based on Minna no Nihongo and Marugoto international standards.

FOUNDATION LEVEL

JLPT N5 & JFT-Basic A1

Ideal for complete beginners with zero background. Covers writing systems and essential survival conversation.

  • Hiragana & Katakana writing & stroke orders
  • 100+ basic Kanji and 800+ vocabulary words
  • Basic sentence patterns (A wa B desu, verb conjugations)
  • Daily life dialogues: time, prices, travel, weather
  • 5 JLPT N5 timed mock examination drills
Duration: 12 Weeks Hours: 150 Hours
PRE-INTERMEDIATE

JLPT N4 & SSW Workplace

Required standard for SSW work visas. Expands compound grammar, workplace Japanese, and reading speed.

  • 300+ Kanji and 1,500+ practical workplace terms
  • Conditional, potential, passive, and causative verb forms
  • Caregiver, airport, and customer service vocabulary
  • Workplace culture, safety signs, and reporting (Horenso)
  • 6 JLPT N4 full-length practice tests with review
Duration: 16 Weeks Hours: 160 Hours
INTERMEDIATE FLUENCY

JLPT N3 Academic & Career

Bridging to professional fluency. Read newspaper summaries, understand natural speed audio, and engage in debates.

  • 650+ Kanji and 3,500+ intermediate vocabulary terms
  • Complex grammar structures and formal Keigo nuances
  • Business email writing and job interview role-plays
  • Listening comprehension at natural Japanese speech tempo
  • Comprehensive N3 examination simulation papers
Duration: 18 Weeks Hours: 180 Hours
COURSE DETAILS

Delivery, Faculty, Assessment & Policies

Transparent standards so you can enroll with complete clarity and confidence.

Delivery Options & Faculty

We provide two active learning modes tailored for local and distant students:

  • In-Person (Negombo): Fully air-conditioned multimedia center at Joseph Mawatha, Negombo.
  • Live Online (Zoom): Real-time interactive lectures with digital whiteboards and recorded video replays.
  • Faculty: JLPT N1 & N2 certified Sri Lankan lecturers with Japan study/work experience, supported by native Japanese guest conversationalists.

Assessment & Certification

Continuous evaluation ensures steady progress toward your examination goals:

  • Weekly Checks: Weekly Kanji and vocabulary quizzes plus bi-weekly speaking assessments.
  • Mock Exams: 3 timed mock exams matching official JLPT/JFT time constraints.
  • Certificate Wording: Students meeting 80% attendance receive an official "Certificate of Completion in Japanese Language Studies (JLPT N5/N4 Preparatory Standard)" indicating completed study hours.

Fee & Payment Policy

Affordable, transparent fee structures with zero hidden surprises:

  • Module-based fee includes textbooks, flashcards, digital audio files, and mock papers.
  • Instalment Plans: Pay an initial registration deposit to confirm your seat, followed by 2 equal monthly instalments.
  • Special discounts available for students combining language with Caregiver or Ground Handling vocational training.

Entry Requirements

Straightforward eligibility criteria designed to help passionate learners:

  • Age Requirement: Minimum 16 years of age (no upper age limit).
  • Prior Language: No prior Japanese required for JLPT N5 Beginner level.
  • For N4 / N3: Prior completion of JLPT N5 or successful passing of our free internal placement assessment.

Official Exam Distinction Notice:

Confiance is an academic preparatory training institute and is distinct from an official examination centre. Official JLPT tests in Sri Lanka are organized by the Japan Foundation and the Japanese Language Education Association (JATAL). Official JFT-Basic tests are administered via Prometric. We provide preparatory training, past paper coaching, and mock assessments; we do not claim official exam-center status or provide unauthorized pass guarantees.

JAPANESE FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions: Japanese Program

Answers to common queries regarding JLPT exams, SSW pathways, and study materials.

How many hours of study are needed to pass JLPT N5?
According to Japan Foundation benchmarks, zero-level learners typically require 150 hours of structured classroom coaching plus home revision. Our N5 program delivers 150 instructional hours with continuous Kanji quizzes and vocabulary retention drills.
What is the difference between JLPT and JFT-Basic?
JLPT is the traditional paper-based exam held twice a year (July and December). JFT-Basic is a computer-based test (CBT) held multiple times throughout the year specifically accepted for SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) visas in Japan. Confiance trains you for both formats.
Are study books and audio recordings provided?
Yes! Every enrolled student receives standard course materials (including Minna no Nihongo practice sheets, Kanji flashcard printouts, and listening audio files) at no extra charge.
Can I combine Japanese Language with Caregiver or Airport Ground Handling training?
Yes! Confiance specializes in dual-track preparation where you learn Japanese alongside vocational skills (Caregiver / Nursing Assistant, Airport Passenger Handling, or Agriculture), ensuring you meet both language and skills test criteria for Japanese employers.
