COURSE FOCUS

Master Hangul, Workplace Safety & EPS Examination Skills

Whether your goal is qualifying for employment in South Korea under the government-to-government EPS (Employment Permit System) or enrolling in Korean vocational academies and universities, our training delivers intensive linguistic readiness.

Who This Course is For

  • Candidates preparing for the EPS-TOPIK examination for employment in Korean manufacturing, agriculture, or construction.
  • Students preparing for TOPIK I (Levels 1 & 2) for admission into Korean universities and colleges (D-2 / D-4 visas).
  • Professionals and travelers seeking practical Korean conversational ability.
  • K-culture and language enthusiasts seeking structured Hangul literacy.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Rapid mastery of Hangul (consonants, vowels, batchim final consonants, and phonological rules).
  • Industrial terminology: machinery operations, safety warnings, tools, and work instructions.
  • Accurate listening comprehension of standard Korean audio clips at testing speed.
  • CBT computer-based testing techniques for the EPS-TOPIK point system.
CURRICULUM LEVELS

Course Modules & Examination Preparation Tracks

Curriculum aligned with the National Institute for International Education (NIIED) and HRD Korea standards.

FOUNDATION

Hangul Literacy & Basic Korean

Designed for complete beginners. Learn to read, write, and pronounce Hangul with absolute confidence within 2 weeks.

  • 14 basic & 5 double consonants, 10 basic & 11 compound vowels
  • Batchim final consonant sound-change rules and liaison
  • Sino-Korean and Native Korean number systems with counters
  • Basic sentence patterns, subject/object particles (이/가, 은/는, 을/를)
  • Introductions, ordering food, asking prices, and basic greetings
Duration: 8 Weeks Hours: 80 Hours
EPS-TOPIK SPECIAL

EPS-TOPIK Examination Track

Specialized track based on HRD Korea official textbooks. Focused on question-paper patterns and computer testing drills.

  • Complete coverage of Standard HRD Korea Textbooks 1 & 2
  • Workplace safety regulations, factory tools, and instructions
  • Reading comprehension question types (graphs, signs, short passages)
  • Listening comprehension audio drills and keyword recognition
  • 10 full-length CBT computer simulation mock tests
Duration: 16 Weeks Hours: 160 Hours
TOPIK I ACADEMIC

TOPIK I (Level 1 & Level 2)

Ideal for learners planning to study in South Korea. Covers extended grammatical forms and academic comprehension.

  • 1,500+ standard Korean vocabulary terms across social topics
  • Compound grammar forms (-고 싶다, -ㄹ 수 있다, -아/어서)
  • Reading short letters, invitations, and advertisements
  • Natural speed dialogue comprehension and inference skills
  • Official TOPIK past-paper drills with performance analysis
Duration: 14 Weeks Hours: 140 Hours

Official Examination Disclaimer:

Confiance is an independent educational training academy offering preparatory coaching; it is distinct from an official examination centre. Official EPS-TOPIK examinations in Sri Lanka are administered directly by the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) in coordination with HRD Korea. Official TOPIK exams are administered by the National Institute for International Education (NIIED). We provide syllabus coaching, CBT computer mock exams, and past paper drills; we do not claim official examination center status or guarantee exam passes or job placements.

KOREAN FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions: Korean Program

Answers to common questions regarding EPS-TOPIK and Hangul study.

How long does it take to learn the Korean alphabet (Hangul)?
Hangul is one of the most scientifically designed alphabets in the world. With our systematic guidance, students typically master reading and writing all consonants and vowels within 1 to 2 weeks.
Do you provide Computer-Based Test (CBT) mock testing?
Yes! Our Negombo training center is equipped with computer stations where students practice real CBT simulation software matching the official HRD Korea interface and time limits.
What is the difference between EPS-TOPIK and TOPIK?
EPS-TOPIK is specifically geared towards employment in Korea under the Employment Permit System and focuses on industrial and workplace communication. General TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean) is academic and used for university admissions and professional visas. Confiance offers distinct preparation pathways for both.
