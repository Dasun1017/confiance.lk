STRUCTURED SERVICES

Our Visa & Document Guidance Services

Clear, step-by-step assistance designed to help applicants navigate complex international documentation requirements with confidence.

Visa Pathway Orientation

We explain available visa categories, government frameworks, and publicly listed eligibility criteria for your intended destination.

  • Exploration of specified skilled worker (SSW), technical trainee, and student routes
  • Analysis of age, education, and language score thresholds (JLPT, TOPIK, CEFR)
  • Transparent assessment of candidate background against official regulations
  • Clarification of timelines, intake schedules, and government quotas

Document Preparation & Application Checklists

Comprehensive document verification guidance, application-form assistance, and certified translation referrals where applicable.

  • Standardized checklists for educational certificates (O/L, A/L, NVQ, degrees)
  • Police clearance report, birth certificate & affidavit format verification
  • Sworn translation and consular legalization referral coordination
  • Precise review of visa application form fields prior to submission

Interview Preparation & Pre-Departure Readiness

Embassy appointment-readiness support, cross-cultural etiquette coaching, and pre-departure briefings for successful applicants.

  • Simulated embassy & immigration interview question drills
  • Target language verbal response practice (Japanese / Korean / French)
  • Biometric appointment scheduling guidance and physical dossier packing
  • Pre-departure checklist: travel insurance, packing essentials, currency rules

Status Follow-Up & Communication Protocol

Status follow-up assistance only where strictly authorized by the applicant and the responsible statutory recruitment body.

  • Assistance in reading official portal status updates & embassy notifications
  • Guidance on drafting formal inquiries regarding processing timelines
  • Prompt handling of requests for additional documentation or clarification
  • Strict compliance with applicant privacy and data protection rights
GLOBAL DESTINATIONS

Supported Country Frameworks

Which destinations and programme categories our team provides verifiable document preparation guidance for.

Japan

  • Specified Skilled Worker (SSW 1 & 2)
  • Technical Intern Training (TITP)
  • Japanese Language School Student Visa
  • Engineering / Specialist in Humanities

Requires JLPT N5/N4 or JFT-Basic

South Korea

  • EPS Employment Permit System (E-9)
  • Manufacturing & Agricultural quotas
  • Higher Education Student Visas (D-2)
  • General Language Training Visas (D-4)

Requires EPS-TOPIK government score

France

  • Technical Trade Pathways (Butchery / Culinary)
  • Long-Stay Work Permits (VLS-TS)
  • French Language Studies & Higher Education
  • Seasonal Worker Visas

Coordinated with SLFEA & licensed agents

Italy

  • Decreto Flussi Subordinate Work Quotas
  • Hospitality, Caregiving & Seasonal Trades
  • Italian Language & University Study Visas
  • Family Reunification Dossier Guidance

Requires CEFR A1/A2 language foundation

Transparent Consultation & Fee Policy

Confiance maintains absolute ethical transparency in all consultation services. We believe that aspiring candidates deserve honest facts without false guarantees or hidden costs.

Free Enrolled Student Orientation

Preliminary pathway orientation and document checklists are included as part of all Confiance full-time language and vocational courses at no extra charge.

Zero Visa Issuance Surcharges

We never charge success fees or visa issuance commissions. Official embassy visa fees are payable directly to the respective embassy, VFS, or government portal.

Statutory Recruitment Demarcation

Confiance conducts technical training and linguistic readiness. Migration employment contracts are handled exclusively by licensed agencies (such as SLFEA) under SLBFE regulation.
