FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before your next step.

Here are a few things students often ask. For advice about your own goals, our team is here to help.

Ask Us a Question
Do I need Japanese language skills before joining?

Beginner-level learners can start with our Japanese language programs. Tell us about your current level so our team can guide you towards suitable preparation.

Can I study online or attend in person?

Students can attend physically in Negombo or learn online. Contact us to check the format and schedule available for your chosen program.

How long does a program take to complete?

Duration depends on the program, starting level and class schedule. Our team will explain the current course plan and expected commitment before you enrol.

Do you help with interviews and documentation?

Our student journey includes interview and documentation preparation. We can discuss CV support, interview readiness and the preparation relevant to your pathway.

How do I choose a country or career pathway?

Start with the Quick Program Finder or a free consultation. Share your goals, education and interests so we can talk through your options and preparation needs.

Does completing a course guarantee a job or visa?

Training supports your readiness. Job offers and visa decisions depend on the relevant employer and authorities, so a course does not guarantee either outcome.
