THE CONFIANCE DIFFERENCE

What Makes Us Different?

We are educators and mentors, not middlemen. Here is why aspiring candidates, parents, and international partners place their trust in Confiance.

01

Strict Training & Preparation Scope

We maintain an honest boundary between educational training and statutory recruitment. We never sell visas or make fictitious employment promises. Overseas placement is managed strictly through licensed partner entities (such as SLFEA under the SLBFE framework).

02

Workplace-Integrated Language Teaching

Our courses teach beyond textbooks. We integrate real workplace terminology—such as Japanese Kaigo (Caregiver) terminology, Airport Ground Handling procedures, Butcher/food processing hygiene, and European hospitality manners—directly into class modules.

03

Real Cultural Etiquette & 5S Discipline

Language alone is insufficient for overseas longevity. We cultivate Japanese workplace discipline: punctuality, proper bowing etiquette (Ojigi), the 5S organizational method, reporting habits (Hou-Ren-So), and western workplace communication standards.

04

Capped Cohorts & Individual Attention

We cap classroom intakes at 15–20 students per batch. This guarantees personalized pronunciation drills, 1-on-1 kanji correction, continuous oral feedback, and direct mentoring from experienced teachers.

05

Rigorous Interview Drills & Profile Polish

Candidates undergo realistic mock interviews with Japanese and European question formats, Japanese self-introductions (Jikoshoukai), professionally recorded video introductions, and standard CV compilation to maximize selection success.

06

Pre-Departure Guidance & Student Welfare

We support students through every stage until departure: travel readiness checklists, banking/insurance briefings, understanding foreign employment contracts, legal rights awareness, and emergency contact orientation overseas.

PEDAGOGY & METHODOLOGY

Our 4-Pillar Training Methodology

A structured progression turning beginners into linguistically competent and culturally adaptable professionals.

Pillar 01

Classroom Learning

Systematic mastery of vocabulary, grammar structures, listening drills, and scripts (Hiragana, Katakana, Kanji, Hangul, French & Italian phonetics).

  • JLPT / JFT exam syllabus
  • CEFR A1 / A2 frameworks
  • EPS-TOPIK question practice
Pillar 02

Practical Instruction

Hands-on roleplay simulating actual workplace tasks, safety protocols, terminology recitation, and service communications.

  • Elderly caregiving simulations
  • Airport passenger assistance drills
  • Food hygiene & butcher safety
Pillar 03

Interview Preparation

Targeted coaching for employer interviews, professional self-introduction formulation, and international-standard CV formatting.

  • Japanese Jikoshoukai drills
  • Candidate video profile recording
  • Overcoming interview anxiety
Pillar 04

Cultural Readiness

Immersion into destination country customs, teamwork ethics, living skills, legal contract basics, and overseas lifestyle adaptation.

  • 5S & Japanese workplace norms
  • European workplace etiquette
  • Pre-departure checklist briefing
