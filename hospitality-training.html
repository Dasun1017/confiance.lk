Hospitality & Food Services Training

Master professional restaurant service, kitchen food safety, fine-dining table settings, customer greeting protocols, and European hospitality workplace communication in French, Italian, and English.

Curriculum & Practical Modules

  • Module 01: Table Setting & Service Etiquette

    European cutlery placement, napkin folding, glassware arrangement, serving from the left and clearing from the right according to formal dining standards.

  • Module 02: Kitchen Hygiene & Food Safety Standards

    Food allergen awareness, food storage temperature guidelines, personal sanitation, cross-contamination prevention, and European health compliance.

  • Module 03: Guest Greeting & Order Taking

    Welcoming international guests, menu presentation, taking detailed orders, handling special dietary requests, and billing procedures.

  • Module 04: Beverage Service & Glassware Handling

    Coffee, tea, and non-alcoholic drink preparation, safe tray carrying techniques, wine bottle opening, and pouring etiquette.

  • Module 05: Hospitality French & Italian Terminology

    Essential culinary French and Italian vocabulary for front-of-house staff, polite dining phrases, explaining dishes, and addressing customer needs.

Practical Lab Equipment

  • Simulated formal restaurant dining tables & chairs
  • Complete European fine-dining silverware & glassware sets
  • Service trays, stand racks & food warmer units
  • Point of sale (POS) billing demonstration tablet
  • Coffee brewing & beverage service station

Learner Eligibility & Prerequisites

  • Minimum Age: 18+ years
  • Education: G.C.E. O/L or equivalent
  • Presentable demeanor, friendly attitude & customer focus
  • Basic conversational English; French/Italian A1 recommended
