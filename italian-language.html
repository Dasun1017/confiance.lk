WHO THIS COURSE IS FOR

Practical Italian for Everyday Life, Hospitality & Migration

Our Italian curriculum emphasizes practical communication over dry memorization. We train learners to speak natural Italian with proper accent, authentic rhythm, and cultural confidence.

Who Should Enroll

  • Individuals joining family members in Italy (family reunification visa requirements).
  • Candidates seeking hospitality, culinary, caregiver, and seasonal work opportunities in Italy.
  • Students preparing for Italian higher education institutions and polytechnics.
  • Anyone passionate about Italian culture, culinary arts, fashion, and history.

Core Learning Objectives

  • Clear Italian phonetics, double consonants, and natural vowel articulation.
  • Essential daily dialogues: greetings, cafe ordering, market shopping, asking directions.
  • Mastery of regular and irregular verbs in present, past (passato prossimo), and future tenses.
  • Workplace communication for domestic assistance, caregiving, restaurants, and retail.
CEFR TRACKS

Italian Course Levels, Syllabus & Modules

Structured progression following the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

LEVEL A1

Italian CEFR A1 (Breakthrough)

Designed for absolute beginners. Establishes essential vocabulary, correct pronunciation, and basic sentence construction.

  • Italian alphabet, phonetics, and pronunciation rules
  • Articles, gender of nouns, and adjectives agreement
  • Present tense regular verbs (-are, -ere, -ire) & auxiliary essere/avere
  • Numbers, days of the week, time, and family members
  • Real-life role plays: at the restaurant, supermarket, and train station
Duration: 10 Weeks Hours: 100 Hours
LEVEL A2

Italian CEFR A2 (Elementary)

Expands conversational range to past memories, future plans, personal stories, and professional daily life.

  • Past tense (Passato Prossimo with auxiliary selection)
  • Reflexive verbs, modal verbs (potere, volere, dovere), and direct pronouns
  • Describing housing, health problems, and doctor consultations
  • Writing simple letters, emails, and filled administrative forms
  • Listening comprehension with native Italian audio materials
Duration: 14 Weeks Hours: 140 Hours
WORKPLACE SPECIAL

Hospitality & Caregiver Italian

Practical add-on module for learners planning to work in Italian hotels, restaurants, elderly care, or home assistance.

  • Caregiving terminology: medications, daily assistance, hygiene
  • Culinary & food service terms: kitchen utensils, ingredients, recipes
  • Telephone handling, taking reservations, and welcoming guests
  • Italian cultural expectations and household workplace norms
  • Mock job interview practice with common employer questions
Duration: 8 Weeks Hours: 80 Hours
ADMINISTRATION & DETAILS

Delivery, Teacher Support, Assessment & Entry

Learn about course logistics, faculty qualifications, and certification terms.

Delivery Options & Faculty

  • Physical Classes (Negombo): Conveniently held at our modern training campus in Negombo.
  • Live Online (Zoom): High-definition interactive classes with native audio clips and digital worksheets.
  • Faculty: Experienced Italian language educators with European language qualifications and long-term residency in Italy.

Assessment & Certificate

  • Continuous oral presentation evaluations, listening comprehension tests, and vocabulary quizzes.
  • End-of-course internal examination covering all four macro-skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking).
  • Official Certificate of Completion issued: "Certificate of Proficiency in Italian Language (CEFR A1 / A2 Preparatory Level)".

Fees & Payment Options

  • Affordable all-inclusive course fee covering all study books and workbook materials.
  • Flexible payment plans: 50% upon registration, remainder in two monthly installments.
  • Zero hidden registration or exam coaching surcharges.

Entry Requirements

  • Minimum age: 16 years.
  • No prior Italian background required for Level A1.
  • For Level A2: Completion of Level A1 or passing of a simple placement interview.

ITALIAN FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions: Italian Program

Common questions about learning Italian at Confiance.

Is Italian difficult to learn for Sri Lankan learners?
Italian is phonetic (it is pronounced almost exactly as it is written), making it one of the most accessible European languages for Sinhala and Tamil speakers. With our systematic phonetics coaching, most learners start reading sentences comfortably within the first two weeks.
Does this course help with Italian family reunion visa interviews?
Yes! Our A1 course covers all standard personal introductory questions, family descriptions, everyday routines, and document explanations frequently required for visa procedures and embassy interviews.
What materials will I receive upon joining?
You receive our customized Italian course workbook, verb conjugation reference charts, audio dialogue tracks, and weekly vocabulary flashcards.
